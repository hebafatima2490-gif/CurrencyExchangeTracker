Mission & Project Overview

The International Currency Exchange Rate Tracker is a web-based platform designed to make currency monitoring and conversion simple and accessible. It allows users to explore the latest available exchange rates for major currencies against the Indian Rupee (INR), convert currencies effortlessly, and analyse historical exchange-rate trends through interactive charts and data tables.

With integrated data retrieval, historical record tracking, and basic analytical indicators, the platform brings essential currency insights together in one place, helping users better understand exchange-rate movements through a clean and user-friendly interface.

Architectural Pillars & Methodologies

1. Mid-Market Benchmark Data

Live rates are retrieved from verified public financial feeds based on European Central Bank (ECB) and interbank observations, providing an unskewed baseline free from retail dealer markups.

2. 30-Day Volatility & Recommendations

Daily prices are tracked in SQLite to calculate rolling 30-day moving averages and percentage standard deviations, classifying market conditions into Low, Medium, and High Volatility regimes.

3. Longitudinal History (2000–2026)

Over 26 years of monthly exchange rates are indexed and rendered with trend-direction segment coloring, allowing exploration across economic cycles and currency milestones.

4. Transparent Fee Auditing

The Forex Decision Assistant evaluates retail quotes against live mid-market benchmarks, decomposing hidden rate spreads, fixed fees, commission percentages, and local taxes.
